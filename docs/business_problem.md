# Business Problem

## Background

Organizations process a large number of journal entries during each accounting period. Reviewing every journal entry manually may require significant time and effort.

## Pain Points

- Manual review may be time-consuming
- Review criteria may differ among reviewers
- Potentially risky transactions may be overlooked
- Review results may not be documented consistently

## Proposed Approach

This project will use rule-based indicators to identify journal entries that may require additional review.

The initial indicators include:

- High-value transactions
- Missing descriptions
- Duplicate transactions
- Weekend or late-night postings
- Segregation-of-duties conflicts

## Expected Value

The tool is intended to support auditors by prioritizing journal entries for further review. It does not replace professional judgment.
